## ggml / llama.cpp

<https://github.com/ggml-org/ggml>, <https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp>

- `crates/straydog-engine/src/iq_grids.bin`: the IQ quantization formats' grid tables, ggml's
  data.
- `crates/straydog-engine/tests/formats.rs`: `dequantize_row_*` ported line for line from
  `ggml-quants.c`, as the reference the decoders are checked against.
- The GGUF reader and every block format's decoding, on the host (`crates/straydog-engine/src/formats.rs`,
  `gpu/cpu_experts.cpp`) and in the GPU kernels (`gpu/`), follow ggml's format definitions.
- The DSpark drafter (`crates/straydog-engine/src/arch/deepseek41/drafter.rs`) follows llama.cpp's
  `src/models/dflash.cpp`, and some sampling defaults follow llama.cpp's `common.h`.

## DeepSeek-V4.1 chat template

`crates/straydog-engine/src/arch/deepseek41/chat_template.jinja` is Unsloth's port of
DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash's chat template (<https://huggingface.co/unsloth/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-GGUF>),
with Straydog's changes. DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash is released by DeepSeek-AI under the MIT License
(<https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017>).
